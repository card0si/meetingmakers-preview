Frequently asked questions

What is Meeting Makers?

Meeting Makers is a GTM engine built for SaaS companies navigating the shift away from what used to work. We solve the age-old challenge of booking qualified meetings by layering cutting-edge AI over decades of proven business development expertise.

How does your AI-powered system actually work?

Our AI operates at the execution layer. It analyzes your best customers to build a precise ICP, scores accounts for buyer readiness, and dynamically personalizes outreach across email, phone, and LinkedIn. This lets our human reps focus only on engaged prospects instead of chasing dead-end activity.

Which channels do you use to book meetings?

We orchestrate three proven channels in sync:

  • Email: dynamically written, personalized sequences using your messaging and proven frameworks.
  • Phone: accurate cell numbers + unique AI-researched value props (500+ dials per rep per week).
  • LinkedIn: warm, referral-style intros via mutual connections and multi-threaded outreach.

All channels adapt to each prospect’s preferred communication style.

Who is Meeting Makers right for?

SaaS and B2B companies ready to stop wasting time on unqualified prospects and start filling calendars with buyer-ready accounts. Whether you’re building a new GTM motion or scaling an existing one, our outcomes-based model dramatically lowers your risk.

How does the prove-it window work?

It’s our no-risk way to test the partnership. You define your exact targets, we launch, and if we don’t hit the agreed-upon meeting volume or quality, you don’t overpay. No long-term lock-in, just results.

How fast can we get started?

Extremely fast. Once you define your targets, we connect the tech and can launch within days, not weeks or months.

Do you replace our internal SDR or sales team?

Not at all. We act as a high-performance extension of your team. Our AI handles the repetitive work while your people stay focused on closing the qualified meetings we book.

What kind of results can I expect?

Our clients consistently see more qualified meetings booked with buyer-ready accounts, higher conversion rates, and dramatically less wasted sales effort. Because we operate on outcomes instead of activity, you only pay for real progress.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We work on results, not contracts. You can pause or stop anytime after the prove-it window. We succeed when you succeed. That’s it.
